# CPD Training

Practical software, data and AI training on the CPD cluster. Choose a lesson from the contents, then run its examples in the **CPD Training** workspace.

[Open CPD Training in JupyterLab](https://icg-cpd-cluster.port.ac.uk/jupyterhub/hub/user-redirect/git-pull?repo=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2Fxangma%2FUKSA_SoftwareDataAI_Training&branch=main&urlpath=lab%2Ftree%2FUKSA_SoftwareDataAI_Training%3Fautodecode).

The workspace includes specialist training software, shared datasets and GPU resources. Your notebooks and other files are saved between visits. Use the notebook's selected kernel for each practical: Python, the machine-learning environment or C.

For everyday research and the Python PhD lectures, choose **Standard** on [JupyterHub Home](https://icg-cpd-cluster.port.ac.uk/jupyterhub/hub/home).

These materials were originally developed for the UK Space Agency Software, Data and AI CPD programme.
